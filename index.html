About

I am a medical imaging AI researcher and educator based in Lagos, Nigeria, holding an MSc in Medical Physics from the University of Lagos and a BTech in Physics Telecommunication from the Federal University of Technology, Minna. My work sits at the intersection of physics-informed deep learning and clinical imaging, with a focus on designing AI systems that perform reliably in settings without the digital infrastructure assumed by most state-of-the-art methods.

My research spans 3D MRI reconstruction from unstructured image archives, accelerated low-field MRI reconstruction, uncertainty-aware ultrasound segmentation, and explainable AI for brain tumour analysis. I believe robust clinical AI must be built for the majority world, not retrofitted to it.

Current research

Metadata-free MRI reconstruction on real-world JPEG archives

Extending the From Disorder to Volume framework beyond simulated archives to real clinical JPEG MRI collections, so that legacy studies held without DICOM or NIfTI infrastructure can be recovered as usable 3D volumes.
