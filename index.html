About

I am a Medical AI Researcher and educator based in Lagos, Nigeria, holding an MSc in Medical Physics from the University of Lagos and a BTech in Physics Telecommunication from the Federal University of Technology, Minna. My work sits at the intersection of physics-informed deep learning and clinical imaging, with a focus on designing AI systems that perform reliably in settings without the digital infrastructure assumed by most state-of-the-art methods. My research spans 3D MRI reconstruction from unstructured image archives, federated learning for disease detection across distributed African clinical sites, and uncertainty-aware ultrasound segmentation. I believe robust clinical AI must be built for the majority world, not retrofitted to it.

As Programme Manager of SPARK Academy, I oversee a pan-African AI and medical imaging training programme operating across 15+ countries, designing curriculum, running Kaggle-based hackathon phases, and coordinating teams across the continent.

I founded NileEdge Innovations, an AI and data science company delivering applied ML solutions across Africa.
